# 🦖 Dino Run

A 2D endless runner game developed using **Unity and C#**, inspired by the classic Chrome Dinosaur game. Jump over obstacles, avoid flying enemies, collect shield power-ups, and survive as long as possible while increasing your score.

## 🎮 Play the Game

**[▶ Play Dino Run Online](https://sreejithhariharan.github.io/Dino-Run/)**

Play directly in your browser without downloading the game.

## ✨ Features

- **Endless Runner Gameplay** – Run continuously and avoid incoming obstacles.
- **Jump Mechanics** – Jump to overcome obstacles.
- **Health System** – Start with 3 hearts; lose health when hit without a shield.
- **Shield Power-Up** – Collect shields to protect yourself from collisions.
- **Flying Enemies** – Avoid birds and other airborne hazards.
- **Distance-Based Score** – Increase your score as you survive longer.
- **Sound Effects** – Audio feedback for jumping, collisions, power-ups, and game events.
- **Background Music** – Retro-style music for an engaging gameplay experience.
- **Game Over and Restart System** – Restart your run and try to beat your previous score.
- **Pixel-Art Visual Style** – Retro-inspired visuals and arcade-style presentation.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Jump:** Use the jump control to avoid obstacles.
- **Restart:** Use the restart button after Game Over.

*Note: Controls may vary depending on the device and build.*

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Unity** – Game engine
- **C#** – Gameplay programming
- **TextMeshPro** – UI text
- **Unity Audio System** – Background music and sound effects
- **Git & GitHub** – Version control and project hosting

## 🎯 Objective

Survive for as long as possible, avoid obstacles and flying enemies, collect shield power-ups, and achieve your highest score.
